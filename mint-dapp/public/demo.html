Three short recordings from a phone, inside the Core app's browser: create your Builder Hub account, get test AVAX from the faucet, then deploy the AvaxCats contract and mint your cat on Fuji. Then a desktop recording: launch your own L1 on Fuji from the Builder Console.

01 · Builder Hub account 0:17

Sign in to Builder Hub

You need a Builder Hub account for the testnet faucet and for Academy badges. Sign in with your email and the one-time code, or with Google / GitHub.

  1. 01Open build.avax.network in Core → Browser, tap the account icon.
  2. 02Enter the Gmail you will use for the workshop and continue.
  3. 03Paste the sign-in code from your inbox. You are in.
Open Builder Hub Testnet faucet
02 · Testnet AVAX 0:18

Get test AVAX from the faucet

The faucet lives in the Builder Console. Switch the Console to Testnet, then request 0.5 AVAX on Fuji C-Chain. Fuji AVAX has no value; it only pays for gas on the testnet.

  1. 01Open the menu on build.avax.network → Console → Testnet Faucet.
  2. 02Switch Mainnet to Testnet at the top, make sure Core is connected on C-Chain.
  3. 03Request 0.5 AVAX on C-Chain and wait for the balance to update in Core.
Open the faucet Builder Console
03 · Deploy & mint 1:03

Deploy the contract, mint your cat

On team1vn.xyz: connect Core on Fuji, deploy the AvaxCats ERC-721 from your own wallet, pick a cat and mint it. The dApp reads the NFT back from the chain.

  1. 01Open team1vn.xyz in Core → Browser, tap Connect wallet, switch to Fuji if asked.
  2. 02Step 01: Deploy contract, confirm in Core, wait for "Contract is live".
  3. 03Step 02: pick a cat, Mint NFT, confirm. Then Register in step 03.
Open the mint dApp Snowtrace
04 · Your own L1 2:05 · Desktop

Create an L1 from the Builder Console

Builder Console → Create L1 on Testnet. Basic setup is a one-click deploy: the Console creates the Subnet, deploys the Validator Manager on C-Chain, provisions a managed validator node, creates the chain and converts the Subnet to an L1. The whole thing takes a couple of minutes.

  1. 01Open Console → Create L1, make sure the Console is on Testnet. Pick Basic setup (recommended) and answer the 4 questions.
  2. 02Confirm the transactions in Core as they come: Creating Subnet, Deploying Validator Manager (C-Chain), Creating Chain, Converting Subnet to L1.
  3. 03Wait for all 8 steps to turn green, ending with "Verifying L1 is Ready". Your new L1 then appears under My L1 Dashboard.
